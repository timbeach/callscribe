When you change a device, what should stick?

1

Per call only

The pick applies to the next start, then falls back to the system defaults. Nothing written anywhere.

2

Per call, with a star to save (my pick)

The pick applies to the next start; clicking the star writes it to the callscribe config as your standing default. Explicit, no surprise persistence.

3

Always saved

Any change is written to the config immediately and used until changed again. Simplest mental model, but a one-off exception silently becomes the new normal.
